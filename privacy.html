Privacy and cookies

Last updated 20 October 2026

This page explains how ZOUND (operated by 1-TO-ALL Co., Ltd.) collects and uses your information on zoundpod.com, under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

What we collect when you contact us

  • Your name, email, phone number, message, and the showroom viewing time you book, if any.
  • The page you sent the form from, and how you found us, such as an ad campaign name or a referring website.
  • What you type in the chat, which is answered by an automated assistant and our team through the Mobile11 contact center.

We use this to reply to you, manage bookings, send quotations and see which channels help customers find us. We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies we use

TypeWhat it doesWhen
EssentialRemembers your cookie choice and makes the chat and bookings workAlways
Marketing: Google Ads, Meta Pixel, TikTok PixelCounts which ads bring you to us and whether you got in touch or booked, and shows you more relevant adsOnly after you choose “Accept”

These marketing providers may process data outside Thailand under their own policies. If you choose “Decline”, the site does not load them at all.

How long we keep it

We keep contact details for as long as we need them to serve you, sell to you and honour warranties. Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you change or clear it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or restrict your data, object to its use, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Committee. Contact us at contact@zoundpod.com or call +66 2 030 3883.
