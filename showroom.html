VOGUE, SR or Life?

Think of it like choosing a seat: SR is a great economy seat on a full-service airline, Life is premium economy, VOGUE is business class. All three share one way of building — no screws, no glue, no wood.

Professional · the original

SR

ZOUND's own design — the shape the market has started to copy. Four sizes, from a one-person booth to a six-person meeting room.

Choose it if

  • you are fitting out an office and want every size in one look
  • you need room for up to six — or eight, in the made-to-order 2XL
  • you want options such as a smart lock or a conference set
Explore SR

For the home · fabric walls

Life

Acoustic rooms upholstered in fabric and set on wheels — a design nobody has copied yet.

Choose it if

  • it is going in a home, a studio or an office watching its budget
  • you want soft, upholstered walls in one of six fabrics
  • it may move — it rolls, and assembles in about three hours
Explore Life

Flagship · one of a kind

VOGUE

Our most refined pod, and one nobody can copy: up to 32 dB of isolation, 3D fluted acoustic walls and a smart control panel built in.

Choose it if

  • the pod is part of how your office presents itself
  • you want room booking, an occupancy light and cloud control built in
  • you want 32 dB isolation in every size, with five sizes and seven finishes
