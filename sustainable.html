What it is made of

Formed,
not fastened.

The emissions figures above are a consequence of the construction, not an additive that was engineered in afterwards.

  • Recycled carbon fibre

    A first for acoustic pods. The shell is formed from it rather than assembled out of boards.

  • No wood

    No ply, no MDF, no veneer — so nothing in the structure carries a glue line.

  • No glue

    Adhesive is the usual source of indoor emissions in flat-pack furniture. There is none.

  • No screws, no bolts

    The shell is formed as one piece. That is also why it goes up in about 45 minutes and comes apart cleanly when you move.

  • Acoustic polyester fibre

    The absorption behind the cladding, in place of mineral wool.

  • Fully recyclable

    A single-material shell is a shell that can be reclaimed rather than landfilled.
