How it is made

No wood.
No glue.

  • Recycled carbon-polymer shell

    Formaldehyde at or below 0.08 mg/m³, TVOC at or below 0.5, benzene not detected.

  • Aerospace-grade aluminium frame

    The structure the panels lock into — no screws through the shell.

  • Acoustic polyester fibre

    Behind the cladding, where the absorption actually happens.

  • Tempered glass

    Full-height, so the pod reads as part of the room rather than a box in it.

  • Motion sensor & power

    Lighting and ventilation wake when you step in. USB 3.0 and 100–240 V sockets as standard; L and XL add USB-C.

  • Options

    Smart lock, conference set, air-conditioning.
