How it is made

Built to move.

  • 6063-T5 aviation aluminium frame

    Light enough to roll, rigid enough to stay square.

  • 15 + 15 mm acoustic panels

    Carbon-polymer board clad in sound-insulating fabric — the finish you actually touch.

  • 3 + 3 mm laminated acoustic glass

    Laminated, not just toughened, because the interlayer is what stops sound.

  • On wheels

    Rated to 100–120 kg so one room can follow you to the next.

  • Infrared sensor

    Lighting and fan switch themselves off five minutes after you leave.

  • 4000 K lighting & acoustic carpet

    Neutral light overhead, absorption underfoot. Life Home adds a Philips desk lamp.
